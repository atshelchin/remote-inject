Privacy Policy

Remote Inject Chrome Extension · Last updated: July 2026

What data we collect

Remote Inject does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information. Specifically:

  • No browsing history — no page content, URLs, or history are sent anywhere. The extension injects a wallet provider into pages but does not read or report their content.
  • No wallet keys or seed phrases — the extension never has access to private keys. It only forwards end-to-end encrypted EIP-1193 requests between the dApp and your mobile wallet.
  • No analytics or telemetry — no usage data is collected or sent to any server.

Local storage

The extension uses Chrome's storage API to save data locally on your device:

  • Your relay URL (default: wss://relay.walletpair.org/v1)
  • The encrypted session state (channel keys and sequence counters), so you don't need to re-scan the QR code on every visit
  • The list of dApp origins you have authorized

This data never leaves your device except as part of the normal encrypted connection to your chosen relay.

Relay communication

When a session is active, the extension connects to a WalletPair relay over a WebSocket. The relay:

  • Forwards end-to-end encrypted frames between the extension and your mobile wallet
  • Can see connection metadata (the dApp's name/URL/icon, both peers' public keys, and each frame's chain ID) but never your plaintext messages or keys — it is a stateless message router that cannot decrypt content
  • Can be self-hosted — see walletpair.org/docs/relay

Permissions

  • storage — saves the relay URL, encrypted session state, and authorized origins locally
  • sidePanel — shows the pairing QR code and connection status in the side panel
  • alarms — keeps the encrypted channel alive and reconnects after the service worker sleeps
  • host_permissions (<all_urls>) — injects the EIP-1193 / EIP-6963 wallet provider into dApp pages so they can request a connection

Third-party services

By default the extension connects to relay.walletpair.org, a public WalletPair relay. Because all traffic is end-to-end encrypted, the relay never sees your data. You can switch to a self-hosted or alternative WalletPair relay at any time in the extension settings.
